The Urgent Reality in Pakistan

Why Your Participation Matters

Pakistan bears the highest breast cancer incidence rate in Asia. OMNIA and APPNA Medical Corps are joining hands to bring free screening, medical aid, and awareness nationwide.

1 in 9
Pakistani Women Affected

Faces a lifetime risk of breast cancer—the highest burden in all of Asia.

~90,000
New Cases Annually

Resulting in nearly 40,000 deaths each year due to delayed awareness and care access.

60-70%
Late Stage Diagnosis
