Health Knowledge Base

Early Detection Saves Lives

When diagnosed at an early stage (Stage I), the 5-year relative survival rate exceeds 90%.

01

Monthly Self-Examinations

Regular self-checks help women become familiar with normal breast tissue, making unexpected lumps or dimpling easier to notice.

02

Clinical Breast Exams

Periodic physical evaluations by a trained healthcare provider ensure professional screening during routine check-ups.

03

Screening Mammography

Low-dose X-ray imaging identifies hidden lesions up to two years before they can be physically felt. Recommended starting at age 40.
